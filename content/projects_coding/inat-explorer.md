## Problem Statement

The iNaturalist website allows people to do basic data exploration of iNaturalist data. However, there are lots of options available in the API that aren't easily accessible on the iNaturalist site.

## Project Details

iNaturalist Explorer is a website that allows people to explore iNaturalist data. I think it is important to give people without coding or GIS experience to explore data so they can look for answers to their own questions.

I built the site as a static site that can run for free on Cloudflare.
